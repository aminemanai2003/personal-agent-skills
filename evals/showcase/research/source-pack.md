# Controlled Source Pack

Use only these sources and bounded claims. Bibliographic metadata was verified through Crossref on 2026-08-15.

## Mack (1993)

Thomas Mack. “Distribution-free Calculation of the Standard Error of Chain Ladder Reserve Estimates.” *ASTIN Bulletin* 23 (1993). DOI: [10.2143/AST.23.2.2005092](https://doi.org/10.2143/AST.23.2.2005092).

Bounded use: The paper develops a distribution-free calculation of prediction uncertainty for chain-ladder reserve estimates under stated assumptions. It supports treating reserve uncertainty as an explicit evaluation object rather than evaluating point estimates alone.

## England and Verrall (2002)

P. D. England and R. J. Verrall. “Stochastic Claims Reserving in General Insurance.” *British Actuarial Journal* 8 (2002). DOI: [10.1017/S1357321700003809](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1357321700003809).

Bounded use: The paper reviews stochastic claims-reserving methods and prediction-error considerations. It supports evaluating distributional and uncertainty properties alongside central reserve estimates.

## Gneiting and Raftery (2007)

Tilmann Gneiting and Adrian E. Raftery. “Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation.” *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 102 (2007). DOI: [10.1198/016214506000001437](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437).

Bounded use: The paper develops the theory and use of proper scoring rules for probabilistic forecasts. It supports using scoring rules that reward honest predictive distributions rather than assessing only point accuracy.

## Wüthrich and Merz (2012)

Mario V. Wüthrich and Michael Merz. *Stochastic Claims Reserving Methods in Insurance*. Wiley (2012). DOI: [10.1002/9781119206262](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119206262).

Bounded use: The book provides a systematic treatment of stochastic claims-reserving methods and their uncertainty. It supports a framework that distinguishes model assumptions, prediction, and uncertainty rather than treating reserving as a single point-estimation task.

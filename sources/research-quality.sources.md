# Research Quality Sources

Inspired by:

- `PM.md` research quality, provenance, and evaluation requirements.
- Addy Osmani's source-driven and doubt-driven development concepts.
- Existing Amine benchmark projects emphasizing reproducibility, assumptions, and separate metrics.
- Official documentation and primary-source practices used in the ecosystem map.

Adapted principles:

- Define evidence standards before collecting sources.
- Seek disconfirming evidence and preserve source scope.
- Separate fact, calculation, assumption, and judgment.

Personal additions:

- Prohibition on arbitrary composite rankings.
- Explicit actuarial/data lineage and uncertainty requirements.

Original implementation:

- The source hierarchy, claim calibration workflow, and examples are original.

# Decision-Making Sources

Inspired by:

- `PM.md` instructions to challenge vague preferences and distinguish taste from quality.
- MADR's lightweight context, decision, and consequence structure.
- Addy Osmani's doubt-driven and source-driven development concepts.
- The repository's inferred preference for narrow, explicit V1 boundaries.

Adapted principles:

- Inspect evidence before enumerating options.
- Record consequences and revisit signals for material commitments.
- Escalate only decisions that genuinely require personal judgment or authority.

Personal additions:

- Avoid arbitrary weighted scores.
- Default to the smallest reversible option that meets the outcome.

Original implementation:

- The comparison dimensions, escalation rule, and examples are original.

# Research Paper Standard

## Before drafting

1. State the research question, contribution, audience, and venue constraints.
2. Build a literature map by argument, method, evidence, and disagreement rather than by paper order.
3. Verify bibliographic metadata from primary or authoritative indexes.
4. Define data provenance, inclusion/exclusion criteria, method, parameters, baselines, metrics, and uncertainty.
5. Create a claim-evidence outline showing which source, calculation, or result supports each material claim.

## Manuscript integrity

- Abstract: report the actual question, method, main result, and bounded implication.
- Introduction: establish the concrete gap and contribution without generic importance claims.
- Related work: synthesize schools of thought and disagreements; do not produce one summary paragraph per source.
- Method: provide enough operational detail to reproduce the work and explain consequential choices.
- Results: separate observations from interpretation and report uncertainty, negative results, and sensitivity where relevant.
- Discussion: explain mechanisms, alternatives, domain limits, and practical meaning without repeating the results section.
- Limitations: identify specific threats to validity and how they constrain the conclusion.
- Conclusion: answer the research question at the supported level; do not merely repeat the abstract.

## Prose audit

- Each paragraph has a claim or purpose that advances the argument.
- Transitions express a real relationship: cause, contrast, qualification, sequence, or consequence.
- Domain terms remain consistent; variation is not introduced merely to avoid repetition.
- Sentence and paragraph rhythm follows the reasoning rather than a visible template.
- Remove broad claims, ornamental citations, inflated novelty, and generic future-work language.

## Final verification

- Open every cited source used for a material claim.
- Verify authors, title, venue, year, DOI/URL, quotation, page, and claimed finding as applicable.
- Reproduce calculations and tables from recorded data and code.
- Check that abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion report the same bounded findings.
- Apply the required AI-assistance disclosure policy for the institution or venue.

# Resume Workflow

## Requirement Map

Create a compact table before tailoring:

| Requirement | Importance | Candidate evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact requirement or capability | Must / preferred / inferred | Project, role, course, artifact, or result | Verified / confirm / gap |

Evidence must demonstrate the capability. A keyword appearing in a skills list is weaker than a bullet showing where and how it was applied.

## Bullet Construction

Prefer this flexible structure:

`Strong action + what was built or changed + relevant context + defensible result`

Useful evidence when a metric is unavailable:

- scale or breadth of the system;
- difficult constraint or failure mode handled;
- ownership and decision responsibility;
- users or stakeholders served;
- shipped artifact, test coverage, reliability improvement, or process eliminated;
- link to a public proof artifact when appropriate.

Avoid adjectives such as "excellent," "innovative," or "highly skilled" unless the evidence makes them unnecessary.

## Parsing-Safe Defaults

- Use conventional headings such as Experience, Education, Projects, and Skills.
- Keep the core content in the document's normal reading order.
- Prefer text over icons for contact details and important labels.
- Use consistent dates and recognizable employer, institution, and role fields.
- Avoid relying on headers, footers, floating text boxes, graphics, or multi-column reading order for critical information unless the destination has been tested.
- Export to the requested format and inspect the extracted text when tooling is available.

These are compatibility defaults, not guarantees. Applicant tracking products and employer configurations differ.

## Final Audit

1. Read only the first third: is the target fit visible quickly?
2. Search each must-have requirement: is there truthful evidence or an explicit gap?
3. Read every number: can the candidate explain its source in an interview?
4. Compare dates, titles, and claims with other submitted versions.
5. Extract plain text from the final artifact when possible and inspect order, missing content, and corrupted characters.

# Definition Of Done

Use the applicable checks, not every check on every task.

## Code

- Intended behavior is implemented and existing behavior is preserved.
- Focused tests cover the changed contract and important edge cases.
- Lint, type-check, build, or equivalent checks pass when available.
- Security, performance, and compatibility risks were considered.

## UI

- The main workflow works at supported viewport sizes.
- Loading, empty, error, access, focus, and reduced-motion states are coherent.
- Text, controls, and dynamic content do not overlap or resize unpredictably.
- Visual inspection or browser verification was performed when the change warrants it.

## Research and writing

- Claims are tied to sources or clearly marked as judgment.
- Assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations are explicit.
- The output is concise, specific, and appropriate for its audience.

## Handoff

- The final diff is scoped and free of secrets or accidental generated files.
- Verification performed and remaining gaps are reported.

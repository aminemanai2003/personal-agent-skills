# Anti-Slop Contract

The anti-slop rules are cross-cutting constraints, not a replacement for domain expertise.

- Do not add visual, code, prose, or process elements without a task-relevant reason.
- Do not claim completion from a single successful command; verify the user-visible contract.
- Do not use generic defaults when the repository, brief, or evidence supports a more specific choice.
- Do not hide uncertainty, skipped checks, limitations, or unresolved conflicts.
- Do not broaden scope merely because a category exists.
- Prefer deleting unnecessary moving parts over adding compensating instructions.
